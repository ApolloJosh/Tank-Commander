# ⚾ Tank Commander

A browser-based baseball **rebuild GM** game. You take over a club that won the World Series four years ago — the stars have aged, the farm system is bare, and you're stuck at a crossroads. **Tear it down, stockpile prospects and draft picks, then rise.** You have six seasons to build a 100-win contender.

**▶ Play it:** `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/tank-commander/`
*(replace `YOUR-USERNAME` after you enable GitHub Pages — see below)*

---

## How to play

Each season runs through five quick phases:

1. **Trade Hub** — Take generated offers or build your own multi-player trade. Sell aging veterans for prospects and picks early; buy stars with your young assets when you're ready to contend. Draft picks are tradeable, and expiring contracts can be extended or moved before players walk.
2. **Set Roster** — Field a lineup of 9 hitters, a 4-man rotation, and 3 relievers. Promote prospects from your farm when they're ready.
3. **Play Season** — Simulate 162 games in a click and see the standings and playoffs.
4. **Draft** — Pick fictional amateur prospects (or "MLB-ready" players late in the rebuild). Top picks have the highest ceilings.
5. **Develop** — Prospects grow, veterans age, contracts tick down.

After Year 6 you get a **final grade** based on how well you tanked, how high you peaked, titles won, and how open your future window is.

A full playthrough takes about **10–15 minutes**.

## Running it locally

It's a single self-contained HTML file with no build step and no dependencies. Just **open `index.html` in any modern browser** (double-click it, or drag it into a browser tab). Your progress autosaves in the browser.

## How it's built

Plain HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript in one file (`index.html`). No frameworks, no server, no build tooling. The league is populated with real MLB player names shuffled onto fictional clubs, alongside fictional prospects and replacement-level players; all ratings, salaries, and outcomes are game-generated estimates.

## Leaderboard

The game has a built-in leaderboard (Top 10 Today and Top 10 All-Time), reachable from the title screen and after each franchise.

By default it's **local** — each player's scores are saved in their own browser, so everyone sees only their own runs. This needs no setup.

To make it a **single shared leaderboard** for everyone who plays your site, deploy the included free backend:

1. Open [`leaderboard-worker.js`](leaderboard-worker.js) and follow the step-by-step setup comment at the top (free Cloudflare Worker + KV, ~5 minutes, no credit card).
2. Copy your worker URL and paste it into `index.html`:

   ```js
   const LEADERBOARD_API = "https://your-worker.workers.dev";
   ```
3. Commit and push. The in-game leaderboard is now global.
